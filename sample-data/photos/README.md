# Your own demo photos (optional, recommended)

Drop real photos here and ClaimPilot uses them instead of the built-in illustrations:

| Folder | What to put in it | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `genuine/` | 2 photos: rear of a car with a damaged bumper / tail lamp (one wide, one close-up) | Ravi's claim |
| `partial/` | 1–2 photos: left side of a car with scraped doors / broken mirror | Priya's claim |
| `fraud/`   | 1 photo of any damaged car | Becomes the photo in an *old* claim; Arjun's claim gets a cropped, filtered copy |

JPG, PNG or WebP. Then click **Reset demo data** in the console (or restart / redeploy).
Camera dates are rewritten to match each story, so photos taken any time work.
